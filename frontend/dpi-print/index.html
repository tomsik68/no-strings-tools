DPI / Print Size

Pixels + DPI → physical print size.

Frequently asked questions

How big can I print a photo?

Divide the pixels by the DPI. For example, 3000 px at 300 DPI is 10 inches (25.4 cm).

What DPI do I need for printing?

300 DPI for close-up prints and photos, 150 for posters seen from a distance.

Is DPI the same as PPI?

For this purpose, yes: both mean pixels per inch of the printed image.
