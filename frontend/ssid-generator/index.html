Frequently asked questions

What is an SSID?

SSID stands for Service Set Identifier — the name that identifies your Wi-Fi network. Devices see this name when scanning for available networks.

How do I change my Wi-Fi SSID?

Log in to your router's admin page, find the wireless settings, and enter the new SSID. Save the settings and reconnect your devices.
