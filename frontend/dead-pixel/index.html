Frequently asked questions

How do I test my monitor for dead pixels?

Click "Start fullscreen test" and cycle through solid colors, looking for dots that don't match.

What's the difference between a dead and a stuck pixel?

A dead pixel stays black on every color. A stuck pixel stays one color (red, green or blue) and can sometimes be fixed.

Does it work on phones and TVs?

Yes. Any screen with a browser works; tap to change color.
