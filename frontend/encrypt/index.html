Frequently asked questions

How do I encrypt text with a password?

Enter a password and your text, then click Encrypt. Share the result; the recipient decrypts it here with the same password.

How secure is it?

It uses AES-256-GCM, a modern standard cipher. Security depends on a strong password.

Is my text sent anywhere?

No. Encryption and decryption run in your browser.
