Frequently asked questions

How do I test my controller in a browser?

Plug it in or pair it via Bluetooth, then press any button. All buttons, sticks and triggers are shown live.

How do I check for stick drift?

Let the sticks rest. If the values don't stay near 0, the stick is drifting.

Does it work with PS5 and Xbox controllers?

Yes. Any controller the browser recognizes as a gamepad works.
