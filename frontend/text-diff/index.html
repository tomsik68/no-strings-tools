Compare strings and text online

Use this free text diff tool to compare two strings, paragraphs, or code blocks side by side. It highlights added and removed lines as you type, so you can spot changes instantly. Everything runs in your browser — nothing is uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

How do I compare two strings online?

Paste the original text in the left box and the changed text in the right box. The diff appears automatically with removed lines in red and added lines in green.

Can I compare code or long documents?

Yes. The tool compares line by line, so it works for code, logs, contracts, or any long text.

Is my text sent to a server?

No. The comparison is done locally in your browser. Your text never leaves your device.
