Frequently asked questions

What resistor do I need for an LED?

R = (supply voltage − LED forward voltage) ÷ current. Enter them here to get the value.

What resistor for an LED on 5V?

For a red LED (2 V, 20 mA): (5 − 2) / 0.02 = 150 Ω.
