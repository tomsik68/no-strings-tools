Frequently asked questions

How do I pick a color from my screen?

Click "Pick a color" and click anywhere on your screen to get its HEX and RGB values.

Which browsers support it?

Chrome, Edge and Opera on desktop (EyeDropper API). Firefox and Safari don't support it yet.

Can I pick colors from other apps?

Yes. The picker works across your whole screen, not just the browser.
