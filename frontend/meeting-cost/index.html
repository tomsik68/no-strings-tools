Frequently asked questions

How much does a meeting cost?

People × average hourly rate × duration. Start the timer to watch it tick up.

What hourly rate should I use?

Use roughly salary ÷ 2,080, or a loaded cost about 1.3× that to include overheads.

Why use this?

To make the cost of long or crowded meetings visible.
