Depth of Field

How much is in focus?

Frequently asked questions

What is depth of field?

The range of distance in front of and behind your subject that looks acceptably sharp.

How do I get a shallow depth of field?

Use a wider aperture (smaller f-number), a longer focal length, or get closer to the subject.

What is circle of confusion (CoC)?

The largest blur spot still perceived as sharp. It depends on sensor size, which is why you choose the sensor.
