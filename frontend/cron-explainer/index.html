Cron Expression Explainer

Decode cron expressions into plain English with this free cron explainer. No signup, no ads, no tracking — works offline.

Format: minute hour day month day-of-week
Example: 0 9 * * 1-5 = Every weekday at 9:00 AM
Special: * = any, 1-5 = range, */5 = every 5

Frequently asked questions

What does 0 9 * * 1-5 mean?

At 9:00 AM, Monday to Friday.

What do the five cron fields mean?

Minute, hour, day of month, month, day of week, in that order.

What does */5 mean in cron?

Every 5 units, e.g. */5 in the minute field runs every 5 minutes.
