Frequently asked questions

How do I measure decibels with my phone?

Tap "Start listening" and allow the microphone.

What is dBFS?

Decibels relative to full scale (0 = loudest the mic can record). It's not calibrated SPL.

How loud is too loud?

Above about 85 dB SPL for long periods can damage hearing.
