Frequently asked questions

How do I stop my screen from turning off?

Tap once and the screen stays awake while this tab is visible.

Does it work on iPhone?

Yes, in modern Safari and Chrome, which support the Wake Lock API.

Why did it stop working?

Switching tabs or locking the phone releases the lock. Tap again when you come back.
