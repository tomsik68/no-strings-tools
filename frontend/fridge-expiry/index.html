Frequently asked questions

How do I keep track of food expiry dates?

Add each item with its use-by date and location. The list is sorted by what expires first.

What's the difference between use-by and best-before?

Use-by is about safety, so don't eat food after it. Best-before is about quality, and food is usually fine after it.

Can I track freezer items?

Yes. Choose Fridge, Freezer or Pantry for each item.
