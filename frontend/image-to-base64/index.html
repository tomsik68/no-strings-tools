Convert any image to a Base64 data URI locally in your browser. Free, offline, nothing uploaded.

Frequently asked questions

How do I convert an image to Base64?

Drop the image in and copy the data URI.

How do I use a Base64 image in HTML or CSS?

Paste the data URI into <img src="…"> or url(…).

When should I avoid Base64 images?

For large images. Base64 is about 33% bigger than the file and can't be cached separately.
