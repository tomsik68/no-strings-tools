Free online speed reading tool

This speed reader flashes words one at a time so your eyes stay focused and you read faster. Paste any article, paper or e-book, choose your WPM, and start reading. The next line preview helps you keep context. Everything runs offline in your browser.

Frequently asked questions

How do I use this speed reader?

Paste the text you want to read, pick a words-per-minute speed, and press Start. Words appear one by one in the center of the screen. Tap or press Esc to pause and exit.

What is a good WPM for speed reading?

Most people read around 200–250 WPM. Start at 250–300 and increase gradually as you get comfortable. Experienced users often practice at 400–600 WPM.

Can I read one word at a time?

Yes. Set the “Words per flash” option to 1 and the tool will show a single word at a time.
