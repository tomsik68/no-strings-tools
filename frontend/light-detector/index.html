Frequently asked questions

How many lux do plants need?

Low-light plants need about 500–2,500 lux. Most houseplants need 10,000+, and full sun is over 30,000.

How bright should a room be?

Living rooms are about 100–300 lux, offices about 500, and detailed work 750–1,000.
