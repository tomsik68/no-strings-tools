Frequently asked questions

What sizes does a favicon need?

Typically 16×16, 32×32, 180×180 (Apple touch icon) and 192/512 for Android. This tool makes all of them.

How do I add the favicon to my site?

Copy the HTML snippet into your page's <head> and upload the files.

What image should I start from?

A square image of at least 512×512 px, ideally simple and recognizable at small sizes.
