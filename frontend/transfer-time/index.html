Frequently asked questions

How long does a 1 GB download take?

At 100 Mbps, about 80 seconds.

Mbps vs MB/s?

1 byte = 8 bits, so 100 Mbps is about 12.5 MB/s.

Why is my transfer slower than calculated?

Network overhead and congestion.
