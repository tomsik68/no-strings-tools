Frequently asked questions

What is box breathing?

Breathing in, holding, breathing out and holding again, each for the same count (often 4 seconds). It's used to calm stress quickly.

How long should I do it?

Even 1–2 minutes can help. Many people do 5 minutes.

Can I use it to fall asleep?

Yes. A slow, steady breathing rhythm helps many people relax before sleep.
