Frequently asked questions

How do I tune my guitar online?

Tap "Start tuning", allow the microphone, and play a string. The needle shows if you're flat or sharp.

What are cents?

1/100 of a semitone. Aim for within ±5 cents.

Can I tune other instruments?

Yes. It detects any note, so it works for ukulele, violin, bass and voice.
