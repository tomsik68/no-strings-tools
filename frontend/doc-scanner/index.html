Frequently asked questions

How do I scan a document to PDF without an app?

Capture pages with your camera (or upload photos), drag the corners onto the page edges, and download one PDF.

Are my documents uploaded?

No. Everything is processed in your browser.

Can I reorder pages?

Yes. Drag the thumbnails, or focus one and use the arrow keys.
