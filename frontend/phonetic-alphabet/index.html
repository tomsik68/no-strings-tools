Frequently asked questions

What is the NATO phonetic alphabet?

It is a spelling alphabet used internationally by military, aviation and emergency services. A is Alpha, B is Bravo, C is Charlie, and so on through Z for Zulu.

What is N in the phonetic alphabet?

The letter N is November in the NATO phonetic alphabet.

How do I convert text to phonetic spelling?

Type or paste your text and the tool shows the NATO code word for each letter. Numbers are read as digits.
