Frequently asked questions

Why keep a headache diary?

It helps you and your doctor spot triggers, frequency and patterns such as sleep, food or stress.

What should I record?

Date, severity, duration, location and anything that might have triggered it.

Is my health data private?

Yes. It stays on your device.
