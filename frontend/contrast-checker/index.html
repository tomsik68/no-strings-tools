Frequently asked questions

What contrast ratio does WCAG require?

AA needs 4.5:1 for normal text and 3:1 for large text. AAA needs 7:1 and 4.5:1.

What counts as large text?

18pt (24px) regular, or 14pt (about 18.7px) bold and above.

How is the contrast ratio calculated?

From the relative luminance of the two colors: (L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05), ranging from 1:1 to 21:1.
