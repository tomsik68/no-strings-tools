Frequently asked questions

What is crop factor?

How much smaller a sensor is than full frame (36×24 mm). Multiply focal length by it to get the full-frame equivalent field of view.

What is 50mm on APS-C equivalent to?

About 75 mm on Nikon, Sony or Fuji (1.5×) and 80 mm on Canon (1.6×).

Does crop factor change the aperture?

The f-number for exposure stays the same, but the depth of field looks like that of an aperture multiplied by the crop factor.
