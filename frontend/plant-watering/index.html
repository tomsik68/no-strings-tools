Frequently asked questions

How often should I water my houseplants?

It varies: succulents every 2–3 weeks, most tropicals weekly. Check that the soil is dry first.

How does it remind me?

Each plant shows when it's next due, based on when it was last watered.

Can I add my own plants?

Yes. Type any name and choose an interval.
