Frequently asked questions

How do I show a message in big letters on my screen?

Type your message and tap "Show it big". The text fills the whole screen.

What can I use it for?

Airport pickup signs, messages across a room or a stage, reading from a distance, or a quick sign at a concert.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Turn your phone sideways for the biggest letters.
